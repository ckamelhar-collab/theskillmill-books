This is for handlers who need something calmer and more structured than generic enrichment advice.

Set up short indoor scent games before moving to harder spaces, so your dog learns the pattern without outside pressure.

Build confidence through sniffing

Use your dog's natural search behavior as a structured confidence habit, not another obedience drill.

Track threshold change

Measure focus, recovery time, and trigger distance so you can tell whether the protocol is helping.
