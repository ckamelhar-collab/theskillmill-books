If you're here, you probably already know this feeling

  • You walk at 6am so you don't meet anyone.
  • You've crossed the street so many times it's just your route now.
  • Your hand tightens on the leash before your dog even sees the other dog.
  • You've been told to "socialise them more" by someone whose dog has never lunged at anything.
  • You've tried treats, harnesses, stopping every time they pull — and nothing held.

Here's the part almost nobody tells you: most of that advice isn't wrong, it's just being run at the wrong distance. A dog over its threshold physically cannot learn — it can't take food, it can't hear you. Every rep at that distance teaches nothing. Find the distance where your dog can still think, and the same techniques suddenly start working.
