Who we are

The Skill Mill publishes practical nonfiction books. This website (theskillmillbooks.com) is our catalog page. It is operated by Howard Kamelhar as a sole proprietorship.

What data we collect

This website is a static catalog. We do not collect, store, or process any personal information directly through this site. No accounts, no sign-ups, no forms, no tracking pixels, no analytics cookies beyond what GitHub Pages may set by default.

Our backend applications

We operate internal server-to-server applications that publish content on our own behalf to social platforms (Pinterest, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube) using each platform's official API. These applications:

  • Authenticate only to our own accounts
  • Do not access, collect, or store any other user's personal data
  • Do not act as intermediaries for third-party users
  • Post only pre-approved marketing content (images, videos, captions) describing our published books

If you interact with our content on those platforms (liking a pin, commenting on a video, etc.), that interaction is governed by that platform's own privacy policy, not ours.

Affiliate links

Our books are sold through Amazon Kindle. Some links on this site and in our marketing content are Amazon product links. We do not receive personally identifiable information about clicks.

Cookies

This site does not set cookies. GitHub Pages (our hosting provider) may set minimal operational cookies; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data on this site, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, or delete. If you have questions about content we have posted referencing you on a social platform, contact us at the email above.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy
