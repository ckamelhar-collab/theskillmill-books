The UK Campervan Legal and Admin Checklist Pack

Free for readers of Campervan Life in the UK for Beginners (Open Road Publishing). No purchase proof, no review, no screenshot required.

Print it, keep it in the van's folder, tick it off

Every sheet below matches a section of Chapter 11 of the book. Each one names the official page it was checked against in October 2026; rules, fees and charges change, so check the page before you rely on a figure. This pack is a set of reminders, not legal or insurance advice.

Readers of the American edition have a separate 12 V wiring-diagram pack at theskillmillbooks.com/bonus/van-life/. Its battery, solar and fuse diagrams apply to a UK van; its mains diagrams are American (120 V) and do not.

Sheet 1 · DVLA “motor caravan” body type application

Checked against gov.uk “Converting a vehicle into a motor caravan” (updated 20 October 2026). The body type change does not affect insurance category, speed limits or legal requirements, and you may use the van as a motor caravan without it if the internal features are met.

  • External: two or more windows on at least one side of the main body (cab door windows do not count)
  • External: a separate door to the living area (a window in it counts as a window)
  • External: a high-top roof or a pop-top elevating roof
  • Internal: seats and a table (the table may be removable; everything else rigidly fixed)
  • Internal: sleeping accommodation (may convert from the seats)
  • Internal: cooking facilities
  • Internal: storage facilities
  • DVLA conversion checklist completed
  • Interior photos of each feature, bed and table in use position, showing two or more windows
  • Exterior photos: front, both sides, rear, plates visible
  • Photo of the VIN plate on the original chassis or bodyshell
  • Description, date and registration written on the back of every photo
  • V5C section 1 (new style) or section 7 (old style) completed
  • Posted to DVLA, Swansea, SA99 1BA; copies of everything kept

Sheet 3 · Insurance declaration prompt

Based on a UK specialist insurer's published guidance (July 2026) and the legal requirement to insure any vehicle used on the road (gov.uk). Give every insurer the same honest description.

  • Base vehicle: make, model, year, registration, plated MAM
  • Conversion completed or still underway (date)
  • Fixed furniture described; estimated value of materials and labour
  • Gas installation: who installed/tested it; certificate date
  • 230 V installation: inlet, consumer unit, RCD; test certificate date
  • 12 V system: battery type and capacity, solar wattage, charger types
  • Water system, roof changes (fan, panels, pop-top), windows added
  • Security: deadlocks, slam locks, alarm, tracker, safe
  • Use declared: full-time home / part-time / holidays; worked from; days abroad per year
  • Photos of interior and exterior; receipts for major components attached
  • Agreed value requested and the figure confirmed in writing
  • Policy start date; renewal date; changes-to-declare reminder set

Sheet 5 · Clean-air zone route check

gov.uk Clean Air Zones (Bath C, Birmingham D, Bradford C, Bristol D, Portsmouth B, Sheffield C, Tyneside C); TfL ULEZ (£12.50 a day, penalty £180/£90); mygov.scot LEZs in Aberdeen, Dundee, Edinburgh, Glasgow (penalty £60/£30, doubling within 90 days). Standards: Euro 6 diesel / Euro 4 petrol. Figures October 2026.

  • Van's Euro status read from the V5C or the free DVLA vehicle enquiry
  • Checked on TfL's vehicle checker (London) before any trip inside the M25
  • Checked on gov.uk/clean-air-zones for each English city on the route; charge paid within the window (6 days before to 6 days after)
  • Checked on vehicleemissionscheck.service.gov.scot for the four Scottish cities (no prepayment possible; non-compliant vans are penalised)
  • Route adjusted or charges budgeted

Sheet 6 · Europe paperwork and the 90/180 tally

gov.uk “Driving in the EU”, “EU Entry/Exit System” and the number-plate identifier page, October 2026.

  • Passport issued less than 10 years before arrival; valid 3 months after the planned return
  • EES: expect fingerprints and a photo at the first border (Dover, Folkestone and St Pancras check before departure)
  • ETIAS: apply only on the official EU site once the EU announces the start date (fee 20 euros)
  • UK identifier: Union-flag UK plate, or a UK sticker; always a UK sticker in Spain, Cyprus and Malta
  • Photocard licence (no IDP needed in the EU); V5C; insurance certificate; insurer's days-abroad limit noted
  • Country kit rules checked on gov.uk travel advice (triangle, vests, emission stickers where required)
  • Gas: refillable bottle and adaptors, or enough UK cylinders for the trip (UK Calor bottles are not exchangeable abroad)
  • Hook-up: polarity tester and reversing lead packed
