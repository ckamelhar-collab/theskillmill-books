What the book covers

It teaches the Layer Method: build one thing, live with it for about a week, then add the next. Home Assistant is free, open-source software that runs on a small computer in your own home and talks to your devices over your own network.

  • Installing on a Raspberry Pi, a Home Assistant Green or a mini PC, using the current onboarding wizard
  • Connecting your first Zigbee, Z-Wave, Wi-Fi and Matter devices in small, testable batches
  • Building your first automations in the visual editor, such as a motion light that does not flicker off while you are standing still
  • Dashboards built card by card, voice control, energy tracking and a DIY security setup with no monthly plan
  • Diagnosing a broken automation with Home Assistant's own Trace and Logbook tools

This third edition follows the current menus (Home Assistant 2026.9) and includes real screenshots. No programming experience is needed.
