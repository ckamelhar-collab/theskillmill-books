Smart Home Setup Audit Sheet

Device inventory

  • List every smart device by room.
  • Mark local-control devices separately from cloud-only devices.
  • Identify bulbs, switches, sensors, plugs, locks, and thermostats.
  • Flag any device with unreliable Wi-Fi.
  • Choose one room as the starter zone.

First automations

  • Motion-based hallway lights.
  • Leak sensor phone alert.
  • Door-open reminder after 10 minutes.
  • Temperature comfort schedule.
  • Goodnight scene for lights and locks.

Reliability checks

  • Use helpers and groups before complex templates.
  • Add manual override logic.
  • Test each automation for three days.
  • Document what failed and why.
  • Back up Home Assistant before major changes.
