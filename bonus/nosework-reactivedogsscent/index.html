Setup

  • Pick a low-distraction room.
  • Use 3-5 boxes or containers.
  • Choose one target odor or treat-based starter method.
  • Keep sessions under five minutes.
  • End before your dog gets frustrated.

Session log

  • Record location, duration, and trigger level.
  • Note confidence, focus, and recovery time.
  • Use easier hides after a hard day.
  • Avoid busy outdoor searches too soon.
  • Celebrate sniffing, not speed.

Reactivity safeguards

  • No forced greetings.
  • No crowded parks for early searches.
  • Use barriers and distance.
  • Give decompression time afterward.
  • Stop if arousal climbs instead of settles.
