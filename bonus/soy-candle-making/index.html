Batch setup

  • Record wax brand, jar diameter, wick series, and wick size.
  • Log fragrance oil percentage by weight.
  • Note pour temperature and room temperature.
  • Label every test candle before curing.
  • Cure long enough before the first burn test.

Burn test

  • Trim wick to 1/4 inch before lighting.
  • Burn 2-4 hours or until melt pool reaches edge.
  • Track flame height, soot, mushrooming, and hot throw.
  • Stop unsafe tests immediately.
  • Change only one variable in the next test.

Common fixes

  • Tunneling: test a hotter or larger wick.
  • Sooting: test a smaller wick or lower fragrance load.
  • Weak scent: extend cure time before changing oil.
  • Wet spots: adjust cooling environment.
  • Rough tops: review pour temperature and cooling speed.
