The part of the build where everyone stalls

You have watched the videos and opened nine tabs, and every one of them contradicts the last. One says lithium is essential; the next says it is a waste of money. So you have bought nothing.

That is a reasonable response to conflicting advice about a purchase that costs real money and can, if you get it wrong, burn your van down. This book was written for exactly that stall.

Chapter 1 has you wire a working circuit today

Under 40 dollars, about an hour, no soldering and no experience: a 12 volt USB outlet that charges your phone from a battery you wired yourself. That circuit is the whole system in miniature. Once it works, the full build stops being mysterious.

What is inside

  • The amp-hour audit that tells you what your van actually uses, so you stop guessing at battery size
  • Lithium or AGM decided with your own numbers and 2026 prices, including the case where lithium is the wrong buy
  • Solar sized for the season you really travel in, with honest winter figures instead of summer marketing
  • Alternator charging done correctly, and why wiring lithium straight to a modern alternator destroys it
  • Wire gauge and fuses using the marine-grade practice serious builders follow, including the 7-inch protection rule
  • Full wiring walkthroughs for a 100Ah weekend build and a 300Ah full-time build
  • Whether you need an inverter at all, and why running a laptop through one wastes power twice
  • A plan for three cloudy days in November, made before November
  • The five-check fault method that finds most problems with a 25 dollar multimeter
